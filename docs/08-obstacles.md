# 8 Obstacles

The main obstacles the player must overcome to win the game include the enemies and puzzles listed in the tables below.

## 8.1 Enemies

| Enemy      | Description                                                                                                         |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Warrior        | Warriors of the fanatical Calydonian group called the Vigil that the PC must fight at key points in the game.           |
| Gladiators     | Dwarves, Humans, Elves, Half-Orcs, Gnomes, or Halflings who are gladiatorial fighters of the Ambracian Colosseo.        |
| Magical Beasts | Gargoyles, Stirges, and other beasts known as “Creeps” and encountered in the lower levels of Prison Darkhenge.         |
| Aberrations    | Ettercaps, Hook Horrors, and other aberrations the PC must fight when engaging in spiritual warfare.                    |
| Undead         | Ghouls, Mohrgs, Wights, Shadow Fiends, and other undead creatures the PC must fight when engaging in spiritual warfare. |
